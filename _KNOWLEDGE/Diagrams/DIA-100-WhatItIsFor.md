# DIA-100: What this repository is for
## Snapshot
- **Diagram file:** `_KNOWLEDGE/Diagrams/DIA-100-WhatItIsFor.svg`
- **Generated by:** `_KNOWLEDGE/Diagrams/gen_site_100_101.py` (edit the generator, then run `python gen_site_100_101.py .` in that folder)
- **Theme:** One folder, four series, and the one road to the public website
- **Primary audience takeaway:** The repository holds four series of Zo's work. Two of them, the website and the resume, become the public site, and they get there one way: Zo sends the changes up, and the publisher does the rest.

## What The Diagram Shows
On the left, a frame for Zo's computer holds the four series as four boxes: the website (essays and an illustrated series), the resume (an interactive resume, with cover letters that stay off the site), the home library (our own AI at home) and checking AI work (plans only, nothing built yet). Under them is a fifth box for the project notes, which are never published. On the right, three boxes run top to bottom and are joined by numbered arrows: the online copy, the publisher, the live site. Green marks what ends up on the public website, white marks what stays off it, and blue marks the two internet services on the road.

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:**
  - Zo's computer (the working copy), holding `anthologies/website-series`, `anthologies/resume-series`, `anthologies/home-library-series`, `anthologies/gatekeeper-series` and `_KNOWLEDGE`
  - The online copy (GitHub)
  - The publisher (Cloudflare Pages)
  - The live site (`alonzo-anthologies.pages.dev`)
- **Relationships:**
  - Step 1: send the changes up (`git push`), from the whole folder to the online copy
  - Step 2: the online copy tells the publisher
  - Step 3: the publisher puts the pages up on the live site
- **Notable labels or metrics:**
  - "a copy of the whole folder, kept on the internet, in public": the GitHub repository is public, so the notes and the two white series can be read there even though they are not on the website
  - "makes web pages from the website and the resume only"
  - "Nothing is uploaded by hand. Sending the changes up starts the rest."

## How To Read It
1. Read the four boxes in the frame: that is what the repository holds.
2. Note the colors: the two green series are the ones that reach the public.
3. Follow steps 1, 2 and 3 down the right side to the live site.
4. The project notes box is where this picture itself lives.
