# DIA-103: From sent to seen
## Snapshot
- **Diagram file:** `_KNOWLEDGE/Diagrams/DIA-103-Hosting.svg`
- **Theme:** What happens after a change is sent up, until a visitor sees it
- **Primary audience takeaway:** Sending the change up is all it takes. The publisher has the pages made and puts them on the internet by itself: the main copy becomes the live site, any other copy becomes a trial copy that is also public.

## What The Diagram Shows
Six numbered steps.

1. A change arrives at the online copy (GitHub) from Zo's computer. The online copy tells the publisher (Cloudflare Pages) that a change has arrived.
2. The publisher hands the work to the page maker (Quartz, run by `build.sh`).
3. The page maker turns the work into web pages, on the publisher's machines.
4. The pages come back to the publisher.
5. The publisher puts the pages up. A change to the main copy goes to the live site, `alonzo-anthologies.pages.dev`. A change to any other copy becomes a trial copy (a branch preview) at its own address, and that is public too.
6. A visitor opens the live site and sees the change.

A dashed grey arrow runs from the online copy to the old duplicate site (`zo-garden`): the same change is published there too, until that site is retired.

Two notes sit at the bottom. If the pages cannot be made, nothing changes: the site keeps showing the pages it had, and no alarm goes off. Nobody uploads anything by hand.

The picture is generated by `gen_site_102_103.py` in this folder. Do not edit the SVG by hand.

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:**
  - The online copy (GitHub), the hand-off box shared with DIA-102
  - The publisher (Cloudflare Pages)
  - The page maker (Quartz, run by `build.sh`)
  - The live site (`alonzo-anthologies.pages.dev`)
  - A trial copy (branch preview)
  - A visitor
  - Left over: the old duplicate site (`zo-garden`)
- **Relationships:**
  - Solid arrows with numbered circles 1 to 6 are the steps, in order
  - Step 5 forks: the main copy goes to the live site, any other copy to a trial copy
  - The dashed grey arrow is the same change reaching the old duplicate site
- **Notable labels or metrics:**
  - "public too: anyone can open it" on the trial copy
  - "No alarm goes off" in the failure note
- **Plain names used:** the shared set only (the online copy, the publisher, the page maker, the live site, a trial copy, the main copy, the old duplicate site), plus "a visitor"
- **Left out on purpose:** Zo's computer and the sending step (DIA-102), the key in the secrets folder that is used only for reading settings, the result the publisher reports back to the online copy, the settings the publisher and the work must agree on (the command, the Node version, the output folder), today's deployment ids, how to undo a bad change, the old shelf (DIA-102), and what the page maker does inside (DIA-104)

## How To Read It
1. Start at the online copy, top left, and follow the circles 1 to 4 along the top row.
2. At circle 5, read the two labels on the fork: which copy the change was made to decides where it goes.
3. Circle 6 is the visitor. Only the live site has one drawn, but a trial copy is public as well.
4. Read the dashed grey arrow last: it is the leftover.
