## Verdict up front

**BUILD AFTER EDITS.** The back of the sleeve has a real spine (the Boeing jump, the refactor, the callback) and the album format suits Zo. Three things would fail review as written:

1. **The palette is the banned look in a new outfit.** Deep navy plus "one small warm point, a desk lamp glow" is a dark background with a single orange accent.
2. **The cover is the Lofi Girl scene.** A rainy night window over a city skyline with a desk lamp is the most copied image in the genre. The small ringed planet is a sticker on someone else's picture. Cover the name and nothing says Zo.
3. **The number count is wrong.** The self-check claims two numbers. The page prints at least six quantities ("8 tracks", "350,000", "Five Teams", "five teams", "A team of five", "7M" twice, "two years") plus seven years and eight track numerals.

There are also four hooks, not three, and two lines that are false or close to it ("Corporate finance" and "Refactored 350,000 lines").

The lightning is the only accent. No warm accent anywhere. The sky is built from flat bands and cloud texture, not a gradient wash." (Fixes two banned items at once: dark plus single orange accent, and gradient wash.)
2. **Replace the cover scene.** Cut "a rainy window at night. This is the one picture only Zo would make: space game, storm, factory and calm in a single frame, and it connects topics rather than listing them. It still reads as lofi through grain, soft focus and the j-card back, not through the rainy window meme.

## Hooks under a skeptical hiring manager

**The Relay Jump (feat. the mentors).** Hardest question: "You left a selective Boeing program for a car-loan shop. That looks like a step down. Why, and what did those mentors change that you could not have learned at Boeing?" **Earns it.** It is Zo's own story, the register backs mentorship through code review at Manor, and the answer is the plot of the whole page. Risk: if the line stays vague ("rewired how I work"), the answer sounds like a platitude. Edit 13 pins it to work ethic, which is his word.

**Maintenance Mode.** Hardest question: "Who did pay? And a 350,000-line refactor usually stalls. Did yours ship, and how do you know it worked?" **Earns it after edit 6.** The register says service-repository patterns reversed negative sprint velocity, which is a measurable answer. As written ("Refactored 350,000 lines") it gets exposed by the follow-up "you personally changed 350,000 lines?"

**Callback (feat. my old CEO).** Hardest question: "Why would a CEO call back a former team lead instead of a firm, and what did you find?" **Earns it, and it is the best proof of the brand line on the page.** The register has the full answer: four months, no prior context, sampled the code against SOLID, tied each structural finding to a KPI miss, delivered phased remediation. Mark it unvetted: Zo gave two stories, and this is the griller's pick for the third from the register.

**HANDS-ON ADVISORY (label, not a hook).** Hardest question: "What does hands-on mean for a Director, and how many hours a week do you actually code?" **Exposed unless Zo has a number-free answer ready.** The register supports it (quest 12: "The founders brought me in for direction. I stayed and built the application."), but the page does not show it.

**Interlude (Interim Director).** Hardest question: "You were interim director and then a CTO was hired. Why did you leave soon after?" **Exposed if Zo has no answer.** It is the one place the page shows director work, so it is worth keeping, but Zo should rehearse this before sending.

**Day One Deploys (if left as a hook).** Hardest question: "Day one of what? A scaffold that deploys an empty app is a template, not leadership." **Exposed.** That is why edit 9 makes it a plot beat, not a hook.

## Identity

**Cover the name: the cover fails, the back half passes.** A rainy window at night over a city skyline with a desk lamp is the Lofi Girl scene. Millions of thumbnails, stream backdrops and Spotify covers use it. The small ringed planet is a gesture, not a connection. Someone who knows Zo would recognize the Mass Effect relay and the "feat." credits on the back, but nothing on the cover.

**Space games, storms and lofi are listed, not connected.** The sketch has rain (lofi), a lightning flash (storm) and a planet (space) as three separate props, then names them again in the credits ("systems thinking, space games and thunderstorms"). That is the same gesture twice. Zo's own trait is connecting ideas from different topics, so the page should do the connecting itself:

- **One picture, four ideas.** The orbital view of a storm over a planet whose lights are a factory layout (edit 2). Mass Effect gives the vantage (looking down at a planet from a ship), Factorio gives the city lights, the storm is his calm, and the distance is the calm: storms look peaceful from orbit. That is also the brand line as an image: from where Zo stands, the hard problem looks manageable.
- **Lightning as the link line** (edit 3). It ties the tracks together the way he ties topics together.
- **Music words that are also engineering words.** "Standards" (jazz tune, engineering rule), "Quiet Storm" (radio format, his calm), "Interlude" (album form, interim role), "label" (record label, employer), "Maintenance Mode" (already does this). Each title should work in both worlds. That double meaning is the identity, and a generic profile cannot copy it without copying him.
- **Press Start 2P on the side labels** (edit 19) ties the sleeve to his resume site.
- **Keep it calm.** No glow effects, no neon. Lofi is soft and grainy.

**Where it still slides into cliche, even after edits:** "feat." on three tracks is a lot; keep it on The Relay Jump and Callback only (the sketch already does). Avoid steam from a mug, a cat, headphones, a figure at a desk, a vinyl record graphic and VHS scan lines. Any one of those puts it back in the meme.

## Album title and sticker

- **Easier Weather.** Ties the brand line ("easier") to storms. Clear, a little flat. Runner-up.
- **Lighter Load.** Generic. Sounds like a laundry or moving ad, and loses the storm. Cut.
- **Calm Front.** A good weather pun, but "putting up a calm front" means faking composure. A hiring manager may read it that way. Cut.
- **Recommended: "Clearing Likely".** It is forecast language, and the brand line itself is a forecast: a problem is "likely to get easier" with him included. It carries the storm, the plot (storm, then clearing), and the honesty of "likely" rather than "guaranteed". It also sounds like a real lofi album title.
- **Other option: "Low Pressure".** In lofi, low pressure means relaxed. In weather, a low pressure system is the storm. A good cross-topic pun, but a Director candidate called "low pressure" may read as low urgency. Use only if Zo likes the risk.

**HANDS-ON ADVISORY** works. It is the one joke that also states the role. Keep it in the black-and-white advisory shape so it reads at 25% zoom, but recolor it to the storm palette's darkest tone. Change the small print to "Contains explicit code" (edit 15).
