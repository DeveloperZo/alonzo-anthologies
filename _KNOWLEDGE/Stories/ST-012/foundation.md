## Two stories (Zo, 2026-10-19)

The original profile works because it tells two stories before the resume is opened and before he speaks:

1. **An equation: who I am.** 01 + 02 + 03 lead to an outcome, 04.
2. **A left-to-right progression: how I got here.**

It is deliberately incomplete. It leaves out experience and conveys intent. Both stories must be on the page.

The equation band sits above the line:

- **01 learn(ai):** Uses AI to learn faster than the stack changes.
- **+ 02 solve(hard):** Goes looking for the difficult problems. That is where the skill gets built.
- **+ 03 direct(build):** Sets the technical direction, then ships against it.
- **= 04 include(me):** Teammates bring me in on hard problems because they tend to get easier with me included.

The brand line is the result (04). It replaces the separate payoff block. The terms quietly echo the acts: learn comes from Foundations, solve from Change, direct from Leverage, and Multiplication is the equation running at scale.
