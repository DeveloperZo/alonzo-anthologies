# Lofi album profile: text sketch v1

Canvas 1600 x 1000. Left: the album cover, 1000 x 1000. Right: the back of the sleeve, 600 x 1000, holding the tracklist and liner notes.

## Look

- A blue night palette: deep navy, rain blue and a pale moonlight tint, plus one small warm point, a desk lamp glow.
- The cover is a rainy window at night. Through it you see a quiet city skyline, and above the city a faint starfield with a small ringed planet (the space nod).
- Rain streaks on the glass. A soft lightning flash lights one cloud.
- Type:
  - the title in a rounded, slightly hand-made display face
  - the tracklist in a typewriter-like mono, like a cassette j-card

## Cover (left)

- Small, top left: ALONZO WILLIAMS
- Album title, big, low on the glass: **Easier Weather**
  - Alternates for Zo to choose from: "Lighter Load", "Calm Front"
- Bottom right sticker, a parody of the explicit-content label: **HANDS-ON ADVISORY**
  - small print under it: "Director content"

## Back of sleeve (right)

Header: VOL. I · 2012 to now · 8 tracks

1. **Rotations** · Boeing · 2012
   Corporate finance, one rotation at a time.
2. **The Relay Jump (feat. the mentors)** · 2016
   Left Boeing for a small loan company. Met the people who rewired how I work.
3. **Maintenance Mode** · Lendr · 2018
   Refactored 350,000 lines and learned who pays for code after it ships.
4. **Interim** · Lendr · 2019
   Ran engineering through a CTO change.
5. **Day One Deploys** · Productive Edge · 2020
   The title said Senior Consultant. The job was architect.
6. **Five Teams, One Groove** · Inspire11 · 2024
   One architecture, five teams playing it.
7. **7M** · Progyny · 2025
   A team of five, a platform for 7M lives, AI in the rhythm section.
8. **Callback (feat. a former CEO)** · bonus track
   He called me back two years after I left.

**Vol. II** · unreleased · Director, still hands-on.
