32. <span id="part1-ref-32"></span>Anthropic, "Project Glasswing," Anthropic research blog, May 2026. https://www.anthropic.com/research/glasswing. Program update (late May 2026): 10,000+ high/critical vulnerabilities surfaced across ~50 partner organizations; Cloudflare alone reported ~2,000. https://www.anthropic.com/research/glasswing-initial-update. At least 129,000 verified vulnerabilities reported by partners April-July 2026, more than 33,000 rated critical or high; self-reported, from 33 partner survey responses, described by Anthropic as a likely undercount. Glasswing folded into the expanded program.

33. <span id="part1-ref-33"></span>Fronsdal, M. et al. Tests whether providing evaluators with real deployment resources (actual system prompts, production codebases) reduces model eval-awareness; finds audit transcripts remain machine-detectable in the majority of cases. "We did not detect any instances of spontaneous research sabotage." ### Section 8: Leverage examples (Meadows analogs)

38. <span id="part1-ref-38"></span>NASA Aviation Safety Reporting System, "ASRS Immunity Policies," NASA Ames Research Center. https://asrs.arc.nasa.gov/overview/immunity.html. FAA program page: https://www.faa.gov/newsroom/aviation-voluntary-reporting-programs-1

39. <span id="part1-ref-39"></span>C. Seligman and J. M. Darley, "Feedback as a means of decreasing residential energy consumption," *Journal of Applied Psychology*, 62(4), 363-368 (1977). https://psycnet.apa.org/record/1978-11313-001. Twin Rivers, NJ field experiment: in-home display showing daily consumption against a predicted baseline yielded ~10.5% reduction over one month in 29 all-electric townhomes during summer AC season. Becker (1978) found 4.5% from feedback alone, rising to 15.1% when paired with a 20% savings goal. Meta-analyses (Darby 2006; Karlin et al. 2015) place typical feedback savings at 4-12%.

40. <span id="part1-ref-40"></span>Jianping Zhou, "Danish for All?
