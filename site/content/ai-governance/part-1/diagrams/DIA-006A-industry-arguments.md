# DIA-006A: A Proof, Marked for Omissions
## Snapshot
- **Diagram file:** `site/content/ai-governance/part-1/diagrams/DIA-006A-industry-arguments.svg`
- **Theme:** Intent versus loop
- **Primary audience takeaway:** The industry's strongest claims are not struck out. Each is left standing as printed, and what it leaves out is written beside it.

## What The Diagram Shows
An engraved plate of a printer's proof sheet with a red pencil lying on it, in the tradition of a corrected galley proof. Five industry claims are printed down the left column as quotations, each under a short heading and followed by its attribution. No quotation is struck through. Each ends in a red caret, the proof-reader's mark for something left out, and the omission is written level with it in the right margin in red pencil, with one or two lines of support in ink.

Claim sentence: the reader should see that the structural reading does not rebut the industry claims but adds what each leaves out, and a marked proof shows it because a proof-reader's caret deletes nothing: it marks the place where something is missing and puts the missing words in the margin.

Route taken: this is a real printed form, not a metaphor. The content is sentences, so it is typeset as a page. The only drawn objects are the sheet and the pencil. The working plate is `_KNOWLEDGE/Diagrams/proposals/PLATE-006A-marked-proof.svg`.

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:**
  - The sheet, with a turned corner and a hatched shadow, and two column heads: `as printed` and `in the reader's pencil`.
  - Five entries, printed column then margin:
    - `I. PESSIMISTS HAVE ALWAYS BEEN WRONG`. Marc Andreessen, a16z, June 2023. Margin: "The structural issue isn't the prediction. It's whether the system can catch its own failures."
    - `II. AI CREATES MORE JOBS THAN IT DESTROYS`. Marc Benioff, Salesforce, 2025. Margin: "The aggregate figure is probably correct, and almost beside the point."
    - `III. LABS GENUINELY CARE ABOUT SAFETY`. Anthropic mission. Margin: "The safety teams are real. The loop is the problem, not the people."
    - `IV. OPEN-SOURCE DEMOCRATIZES AI`. Yann LeCun, Lex Fridman #416, March 2024. Margin: "The access argument is real. The power argument is more complicated."
    - `V. SLOWING DOWN HANDS THE LEAD TO RIVALS`. Dario Amodei, "Machines of Loving Grace," October 2024. Margin: "The race is treated as exogenous. It is being constructed."
  - The red pencil at the foot of the margin column.
- **Relationships:**
  - Printed text is the claim in its author's words. Red is the reader's hand. The caret ties one to the other.
  - Rust is used for one thing only, the reader's marks: the five carets, the five margin sentences and the pencil lead.
- **Notable labels or metrics:**
  - Every heading, quotation, attribution and margin sentence is carried over verbatim from the earlier card diagram. Months are spelled out (`June`, `March`, `October`).
  - Support lines are cut down from the earlier cards and add nothing new: SEC, OSHA and FDA as oversight that grew with what it regulated, CAISI at $10M for FY2026, the pointer to DIA-006B.
  - Left out, compared with the earlier diagram: the "adjacent" names after each attribution (Pinker, Cowen, Altman, the WEF figure, Acemoglu and Restrepo, the OpenAI Charter, Hassabis, the RSP revision, Zuckerberg, Mistral, Schmidt, Helberg), the AISI renaming date, the "under 10% of one frontier training run" comparison, the 40 year and 20 to 30 year figures (now on DIA-006B), the Petri donation, the sovereignty point, DeepSeek-R1 and the Llama distillation chain, and the "months behind, months ahead" line.

## How To Read It
1. Read a quotation in the left column, and who said it.
2. Find the red caret at its end.
3. Read the red sentence beside it in the margin, then the line of support under it.
4. Note that nothing printed has been crossed out.

## Governance Relevance
Section 6 says the structural response "isn't a rebuttal. It's a different level of analysis." The industry positions describe what individuals intend. The margin describes how the loop behaves regardless. A marked proof keeps both on one sheet without making either cancel the other.
