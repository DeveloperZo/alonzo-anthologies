# DIA-007A: A Wall in Six Lengths
## Snapshot

- **Theme:** The six binding constraints and whether each one holds
- **Primary audience takeaway:** None of the six constraints stands whole. Two are old walls being gone through, two are new walls not yet raised, and two were never walls at all.

## What The Diagram Shows
An engraved plate of one stone wall seen from the front, in the tradition of survey elevations and builders' plates. The wall is divided into six lengths, one for each constraint, read left to right. It starts as old masonry at full height, becomes new footings, and ends as pegs, string and bare ground. The section's own definition supplies the object: a binding constraint is "a physical, legal, or economic wall rather than a posted speed limit."

Claim sentence: the reader should see that not one of the six constraints currently binds, and a wall shows it because a wall only holds where it stands unbroken at full height, and each length here is breached, cracked, unfinished or absent.

## Governance Relevance
The section argues that asking whether rules exist is the wrong question. The useful question is whether anything actually limits what is possible. The plate answers it for all six at once, and keeps the prose's three patterns visible: incidental physical limits being routed around, legal mechanisms not yet activated, and two structural facts that no wall answers.

What the plate leaves out, compared with the status grid it replaces: the PJM auction shortfall of 6,625 MW, the firm names behind the power agreements, the Super Micro case name and the Huawei chip count, the named lawsuits and the $400K fines total, the EU penalty amounts and standards timeline, the named open-weights models and benchmark gap, the 25 percent traceability figure and the NP-hardness result. The old grid said EU penalties "apply Aug 2026" as a future date. The plate follows the prose, which reports the date as passed. The deferral of high-risk obligations to the end of 2027 is in the prose and not on the plate.

The order of the last two lengths is swapped against the prose (interpretability before open weights) so the wall ends in open ground.
