# DIA-005: A Bucket Under the Leak
## Snapshot

- **Theme:** Shifting the burden
- **Primary audience takeaway:** While the bucket catches the drip, nobody is made to mend the joint.

## What The Diagram Shows
An engraved plate of a heavy pipe hung from a ceiling and running off both sides of the frame. At a flanged joint it leaks. Four drops fall into a bucket standing on a dry floor. To the right, on the same floor, lie a repair clamp and a spanner, drawn in rust and unused.

Claim sentence: the reader should see that visible safety work can soak up the pressure for structural fixes, and a bucket under a leaking pipe shows it because while the bucket keeps the floor dry, nothing forces anyone to mend the joint.

Tradition: plumbing and steam-fitting trade plates.

Working plate: `_KNOWLEDGE/Diagrams/proposals/PLATE-005-bucket.svg`. Hand-drawn, no generator.

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:** three mappings, each given by a note beside the part, in section 5's own words.
  - The leak: `harm, once it shows`.
  - The bucket: `safety research published, safety policies announced`. Under it: `It works. The floor stays dry, and nobody sends for the fitter.`
  - The clamp and spanner, in rust: `the mend, never fitted: liability law, mandatory audits, hard deployment gates`.
- **Relationships:**
  - The bucket is directly under the joint and nearly full. The floor around it is dry.
  - The clamp is the size of the pipe. It would fit.
- **Notable labels or metrics:** none. The plate is structural.

## How To Read It
1. Start at the joint and follow the drops down.
2. Read the bucket's note.
3. Look right, to the clamp and spanner on the floor.

## Governance Relevance
The plate sits directly above the paragraph that begins "Watch what this structure predicts. Safety research gets published. Safety policies get announced. The pressure for structural reform (liability law, mandatory audits, hard deployment gates) drops." It draws that paragraph and nothing else. Meadows' name for the pattern, shifting the burden, is in the caption.

The plate does not say the bucket is fake. The prose says the research is not fake, and the note says "It works." The point is that it works just well enough.

What it leaves out: both self-amplifying loops, the open-weights stock, and the section's verdict. The prose carries them. The plate has no R1, B or R2 letters.
