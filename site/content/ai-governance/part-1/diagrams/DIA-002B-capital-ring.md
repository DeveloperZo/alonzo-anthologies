## What The Diagram Shows
An engraved plate of four toothed wheels meshed in a closed ring, in the tradition of millwork and mechanics-textbook plates. Two large wheels sit left and right, two small ones above and below. They are named `LABS`, `INVESTORS`, `HYPERSCALERS` and `NEOCLOUDS`. An arrow beside each wheel shows its direction, and neighbours turn opposite ways, as meshed gears must. One tooth, seated between two teeth of the next wheel, is drawn in rust.

Claim sentence: the reader should see that no actor in the capital loop can slow down alone, and a closed ring of meshed gears shows it because each wheel is turned by one neighbour and turns the next, so a wheel that stops takes the force of the others on its own teeth.

This is the claim made by the prose on either side of the embed: "the structure commits every actor in the loop: each profits from the loop continuing, and restructuring costs fall hardest on whoever stops first", and "the difficulty here is not moral. It is mechanical." The file name still says "capital ring", and the plate is a ring again. The previous SVG under this name drew a different argument (see what the plate leaves out).

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:**
  - Four wheels. The names are the tiers section 2 uses: the lab tier, the major hyperscalers, the neocloud layer, and the investors (the prose says the same companies "invest in each other, sell to each other").
  - Direction arrows, one per wheel.
  - Rust: one tooth at the mesh between the hyperscaler and neocloud wheels, its leader, and the note `Stop one wheel, and its teeth take the force of the other three.`
- **Relationships:**
  - A mesh stands for buying from, selling to or investing in the neighbour.
  - Turning stands for growth.
  - The order of the wheels around the ring and their sizes carry no data. No wheel is singled out as the one that stops: the rust tooth marks where the load would land, not who is at risk.
- **Notable labels or metrics:**
  - None. The plate carries no numbers.
  - Geometry, for anyone revising it: module 10, 24 and 16 teeth, centres on a square of side 200 turned 45 degrees. Both tooth counts divide by four, which is what lets every mesh in the closed ring seat a tooth in a gap.
- **What the plate leaves out:**
  - The whole of the previous SVG's argument, "why even dissent funds the loop": the expected and actual paths of a safety concern, Anthropic's founding in 2021, and the $900 billion and $852 billion valuations. Section 2's prose no longer makes that argument or cites those figures.
  - The earlier spec's force diagram (continuation forces against brake forces). The brakes are the subject of section 4.
  - Company names. Stargate's partners (OpenAI, SoftBank, Oracle, MGX) and CoreWeave are in the prose, not on the plate.
  - The eighteen-wheeler in the sentence after the embed.

## How To Read It
1. Pick any wheel and follow its arrow.
2. See that its teeth drive the next wheel the other way, and so on round the ring back to the start.
3. Read the rust note, then imagine holding one wheel still.

## Governance Relevance
Section 2 ends by saying the difficulty is mechanical, not moral. This plate is that sentence drawn. It sets up section 3's question (who is positioned to slow the loop) by showing why the answer is not "any one of the firms inside it".
