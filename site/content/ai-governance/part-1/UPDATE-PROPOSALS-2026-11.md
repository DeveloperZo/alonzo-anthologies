## I. Source-bias read-through (November 10, 2026)

Question asked: does the piece paint AI or the labs in a flattering light because of where its evidence comes from?

Disclosure: this read was done by an Anthropic model. Anthropic is the most-cited organization in the piece, so the judgments on Anthropic passages below deserve a second pair of eyes.

**I1. The source mix leans on the labs.** About a fifth of the entries in `sources.md` are written by Anthropic or rest on its tools (1, 9, 11, 13e, 31, 32, 33, 34, 36, 37, 42). Capability forecasts come from a group whose scenario assumes transformative capability. No source is a capability skeptic, a labor organization or a plaintiff.
Decision: (a) add one independent or adversarial source per section where one exists (C7.2's UCL survey is the ready example for section 7); (b) add a one-line note on the sources page stating the lean; (c) both.

**I2. Capability claims taken at the vendor's word. Fixed where it was attribution only.**
