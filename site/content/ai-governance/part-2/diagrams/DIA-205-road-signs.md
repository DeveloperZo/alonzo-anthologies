## What The Diagram Shows
A road-safety poster. A road bends to a river bridge whose middle span is gone. Tyre tracks run up the road, past three signs, to the broken edge, and rings spread on the water below. The first sign is a warning triangle swallowed by a tree. The second reads PLEASE CONSIDER SLOWING. The third, in rust, reads BRIDGE CERTIFIED SAFE, with a plate beneath: inspected by its builder.

Claim sentence: the reader should see that a warning nobody can see, a rule nobody must follow and a certificate written by the certified all fail together, and a road shows it because the tracks pass all three and end at the gap.

Manner: a sign-painter's road-safety poster. Flat fills, one pinhole camera (vanishing point 338, 200), no hatch pattern. Uses a few flat tones mixed from ink and paper beyond the four palette colours.
