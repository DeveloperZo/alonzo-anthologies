## What The Diagram Shows
A high-jump pit in perspective. Two tall standards carry a painted band high up, and a ghost of the bar hangs between the bands. The bar itself, in rust, rests on pegs barely above the ground, with its shadow under it. The two pulled pegs lie on the grass by the near standard. Behind is a low sand pit and open field.

Claim sentence: the reader should see that a self-set safety standard was lowered by the party it bound, and a high-jump bar shows it because whoever sets the pegs decides what counts as clearing it.

Manner: a mid-century athletics coaching manual illustration. Two-point perspective, stipple shading, no hatch pattern.
