## What The Diagram Shows
The most general picture of the set, redrawn on 2026-11-18 after Zo decided that the home AI is always in front and paid AI sits behind a switch.

You open one of two pages on the computer at home. Private chat (`lemonpepper:3002`) is for health, family and money. Everyday chat (`lemonpepper:3000`) is for everything else.

Each page talks to a door. A door is a gateway with no screen of its own: it is what pages and tools send their requests to. Private chat uses the private door (port 4001), which only knows the home AI. Everyday chat, and tools such as the hand-off commands, use the front door (port 4000), which has the switch: home by default, or paid.

Both doors lead to the home AI. The home AI reads and writes the private files and reads the working files.

Paid help is outside the frame, on the internet. n8n Cloud comes in through the private door, so it may ask the home AI and can never reach Claude through this system. Claude is reached from the front door only if you flip the switch, and cannot reach in.

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:** You; two pages (private chat, everyday chat); two doors (the private door, the front door); the home AI; private files, drives C and D; working files, drive F; n8n Cloud above the frame and Claude below it (amber).
- **Relationships:** Green lines are the private side. Blue lines are the working side. The two amber arrows are the only links to paid help: n8n Cloud down into the private door, "comes in through the private door", and the front door down to Claude, "goes out only if you flip the switch".
- **Notable labels or metrics:** Claude is off until an API key is added. n8n Cloud is a subscription Zo already has. It runs on n8n's servers. Zo decided on 2026-11-18 that it may be used with private material and Claude may not, so it reaches the home AI through the private door, which is published to the internet with Tailscale Funnel and has no paid route. The switch is the route named `paid`: in the chat page it is a choice in the model list. What enforces each boundary, and how strongly, is in `client/PRIVATE.md`. Today paid tools are kept off the private drives by file permission rules only; a separate Windows account (`claude_agent`) exists and is not yet in use.

## How To Read It

3. Follow the amber arrow from n8n Cloud into the private door, and the one from the front door out to Claude.
4. Read the two lines beside each amber box.
