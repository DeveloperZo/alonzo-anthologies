## What The Diagram Shows
Two frames. The laptop goes anywhere and holds the Arbiter and a web browser. The computer at home stays on and never sleeps. Five pieces there are kept running by one program, the service runner: the front door, the chat page, the logbook, the live board, and movies and shows. The home AI starts by itself at sign-in and does its work on the graphics card. Between the frames is the private connection, which works at home and away.

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:** Laptop frame with two boxes; home frame with a dashed group of five, the home AI, and the graphics card.
- **Relationships:** A two-way private connection between the frames. The home AI to the graphics card.
- **Notable labels or metrics:** The bar on the graphics card is to scale: the home AI fills 12 of the card's 16 GB. Machine names (limesalt, lemonpepper) are small print. The live board also has a small helper program outside the group that gathers its numbers; it is not drawn.
