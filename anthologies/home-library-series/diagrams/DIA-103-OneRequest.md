- **Theme:** What happens between a question and its answer
- **Primary audience takeaway:** The front door checks the password, the home AI answers, and the logbook keeps a note.

## What The Diagram Shows
Five numbered steps along one row. Whoever asks sends a request over the private connection. The front door checks the password and passes it on. The home AI reads it and writes the answer. The answer goes back the same way. The front door writes a note in the logbook. Two side notes give how long it takes and say that the chat page follows the same steps.

## Elements And Flow
- **Components:** Whoever asks (blue), the front door, the home AI, the logbook (green).
- **Relationships:** Steps 1 and 2 run left to right, step 3 is the home AI working, step 4 runs back right to left, step 5 goes down to the logbook.
- **Notable labels or metrics:** A short answer takes 1 to 3 seconds. The home AI is unloaded after 60 idle minutes, and the next answer then takes 6 to 30 seconds (drawn as "up to 30"). The logbook keeps the full question and answer text; the live board does not.

## How To Read It
1. Follow the numbers 1 to 5.
2. Read the two side notes.

## Home Library Relevance
Shows that every request passes the front door, which is why one password and one logbook cover everything. The one way around the front door is in DIA-105.
