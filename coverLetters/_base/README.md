## Making a company version

1. Cut a branch from `main`: `resume/<company>-<role>`.
2. Copy this folder to `coverLetters/<company>/`.
3. Tailor only these parts:
   - **03 Immediate Actions:** what the company will expect first, researched from the posting and limited to what the portfolio proves.
   - **04:** the career outcome.
   - The "For <company>" tag under the header.
4. Keep 01 and 02 as they are, and leave the timeline alone.
5. Commit the tailored resume on the same branch, so the cover letter and resume travel together.

The rules for these boards are in `_KNOWLEDGE/Stories/ST-012/foundation.md`:
- conversation starter, not conversation dictator
- say each idea once
- every line earns its place

The GTreasury version on `resume/gtreasury-engineering-manager` is the worked example.
