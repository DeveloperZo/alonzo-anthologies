Working notes for the August 4, 2026 currency pass on Part 2 (sections 0 and 1, the only drafted sections). Not for publication. Companion doc: part-1/RATIONALE-2026-08.md, which lists the research process and the six resolved conflicts.

## Voice constraint (standing)

All Part 2 prose is written under the information-presentation voice: Angelou cadence and embodied specificity, Muller demonstrative warmth, no em dashes, no signposting, value judgments owned and backed. Edits below were written to that constraint; if a future change reads as wire copy, that is the regression to check first.

## Changes applied

### Section 0 (Frustrated About AI?)

**Amazon sentence added to the earnings-call paragraph.** "Amazon opens 2026 by cutting sixteen thousand corporate jobs and naming AI in the announcement." Present tense matches the paragraph's established rhythm (Salesforce announces, Microsoft tells). Amazon is the largest single AI-cited cut on record and keeps the paragraph's evidence fresh without displacing the Benioff "less heads" anchor, which stays because the quote is irreplaceable.

**RSP beat extended.** After "three revisions, gone": "A fourth revision arrived in April 2026. The pause stayed gone." Two short beats, consistent with the fragment style of the paragraph. v3.1 (April 2, 2026) clarified definitions without reinstating the pause; the addition preempts a reader checking the RSP page and finding a version the essay doesn't mention.

### Section 1 (Beneath the Search Result)

**Macro paragraph rebuilt with August 2026 figures.** Changes: Nvidia "most valuable company" → "first company in history worth five trillion dollars; by the summer of 2026 it was trading the title back and forth with Apple" (the flat superlative was no longer reliable day-to-day; the five-trillion first is durable). Anthropic "$183 billion" → "$183 billion to $965 billion in under a year" (Series G $380B in February, Series H $965B closed May 28; the nine-month jump is itself the story). Capex "$300 billion" → "roughly seven hundred billion, nearly double the year before" (combined guidance, deliberately phrased as "planned"). Slok updated: AI now roughly half of US GDP growth, and his own July "painful repricing" warning added. The warning strengthens the closing line "A contraction would not be quiet" because the system's own economist is saying it.

**Munich ruling added to the harms paragraph.** "In May 2026 a Munich court held Google liable for what an Overview said about a business, the first ruling of its kind. Google is appealing." First court to hold Google directly liable for Overview content; internationalizes the harm evidence beyond Wolf River (which was remanded to Minnesota state court in January and is still pending; the prose's "sued Google in March 2025" claim remains accurate as stated).

**Copilot pricing extended.** July 2026 price rise (up to 43% on some plans, Copilot folded into base subscriptions) appended to the Microsoft sentence. The escalation pattern (bundle, price, price again) is the consent theme compounding.

**Bartz settlement added to the IP paragraph.** Final approval July 20, 2026, $1.5B, largest copyright settlement on record. Placed after Carreyrou (the settlement resolves the class his suit typified) and before the Altman juxtaposition. New closing beat added after "What was taken from them they called theft": "When the bill finally came due, it was priced at three thousand dollars a book." The per-work figure (~$3,000) carries the value judgment: the price of an author's book, set after the fact, without permission having ever been asked. The figure appears only in the closing line by design; an earlier draft had it twice.
